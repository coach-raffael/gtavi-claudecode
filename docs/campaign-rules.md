# GameInsight-GTA: Campaign Rules

Collected 2026-11-19. The machine-readable version for the VPS clip desk is `server/campaigns.yml`.

## Pre-post checklist (applies to every clip)

- [ ] 9:16 vertical, English captions/subtitles
- [ ] Source footage is from the campaign streamer's own GTA 6 stream/VOD and inside the allowed date range
- [ ] The streamer is the main focus for the whole clip, not just the intro
- [ ] Hook within the first 1–2 seconds
- [ ] Our own edit, no reposting another clipper's cut
- [ ] Caption and thumbnail match what's in the clip; every clip gets its own thumbnail
- [ ] Required hashtags and caption text added (see below)
- [ ] YouTube "includes paid promotion" box ticked. These are paid placements, and German law requires ads to be labelled even if the campaign doesn't ask.
- [ ] No AI voices or footage, no fake reactions. Automatic subtitles are fine.
- [ ] Leave the clip public for at least 30 days, with likes visible
